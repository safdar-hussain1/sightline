Sightline

A steering model you can watch drive. It runs in your browser on recorded road footage, turns its wheel beside the real driver’s, and shows where on the road it looks.

Built by Safdar Hussain Source code on GitHub, under the MIT licence.

For research and education only. Neither the model nor the data may be used to steer or control a vehicle.

Driving footage and steering angles from Sully Chen’s driving datasets, under the MIT licence.
